## 📖 Overview

ZimSmart Grow is a web platform that puts practical, AI-driven farming intelligence into the hands of smallholder farmers across Zimbabwe. It combines agronomic data, machine learning, and a farmer-first interface to help growers make better decisions — from what to plant and when, to diagnosing crop diseases early, to finding the best market for their harvest.

The goal: **increase yields, reduce losses, and improve incomes** for the farmers who need it most.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🌾 **Planting Guidance** — Personalized recommendations on what to plant and when, based on location, soil, and season.
- 🔬 **Crop Disease Diagnosis** — Upload a photo of a crop and get an AI-assisted diagnosis with treatment suggestions.
- 📈 **Market Access** — Connect farmers to buyers and current market prices for their produce.
- 🌦️ **Weather & Climate Insights** — Localized weather data to inform irrigation, planting, and harvest timing.
- 📱 **Mobile-First Design** — Built to work well on the phones farmers actually use.
